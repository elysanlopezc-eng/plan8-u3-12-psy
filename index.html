Integrated Case Study & Team Solutions (30 minutes)

Collaborative Work

Scenario: Apex Marketing Crisis

"A fast-growing creative marketing agency faces high employee turnover, interdepartmental conflict between sales and design teams, lagging motivation, and an ambiguous performance review process. Furthermore, a recent campaign pushed ethical boundaries."

Your Team Task (15 min analysis + 15 min presentation):

  • Identify root causes using I/O psychology & emotional intelligence principles.
  • Design a structured intervention incorporating KPIs, conflict mediation, and ethical leadership.

Plenary Presentation Guidelines

  • Structure: Outline the diagnosis, proposed strategy, and expected measurable outcomes.
  • Professional Tone: Use advanced B2-C1 ESL marketing and psychological vocabulary.
  • Q&A Defense: Respond constructively to peer and teacher queries.
